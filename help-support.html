How to get help faster

  1. Open the relevant section above and check whether the issue can be resolved there.
  2. Write down what you were trying to do, what happened, and the approximate time.
  3. For payment issues, include the transaction reference and amount. Never send card PINs, passwords, OTPs, wallet seed phrases or private keys.
  4. Email the support team from an address you can access. Include your Monarch UID if it is safe and relevant to the request.

Frequently asked questions

I cannot sign in. What should I do?

Check that your email or username and password are entered correctly. If you have forgotten your password, use Password recovery above. If you still cannot access your account, contact official support and describe the error. Do not send your password or verification code.

My verification or account request is still pending.

Open the relevant verification page to check the current status and whether more information is requested. If the status appears incorrect or has not changed, contact support with your Monarch UID and the date you submitted the request.

My deposit is pending or I need help with a transaction.

Check your transaction history and keep your payment receipt or reference. Send support the transaction reference, amount, payment method and approximate date. Never make a second payment just because the first one is delayed unless official instructions confirm it is necessary.

How do I report a website or app problem?

Use Feedback & suggestions above. Mention the page or app screen, the action you took, the error message, and whether the problem happens every time. You may attach a screenshot after removing private financial information and security codes.

Can support guarantee trading or investment profits?

No. Trading and investing involve risk, and profits are never guaranteed. Support can help with account and platform questions, but no message, signal or strategy can promise a market outcome. Only risk money you can afford to lose.

How will I know a support message is official?

Use the contact details shown on this page and inside the official Monarch Codex platform. The team will never need your password, OTP, wallet seed phrase or private key. Be cautious of anyone promising guaranteed returns or asking you to pay into a personal account.

When contacting support: include your Monarch UID, the page or feature involved, and a short description of the issue. Do not send your password, PIN, banking password, or OTP. For financial issues, include the transaction reference if available.
