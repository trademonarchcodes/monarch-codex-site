Terms & policies

We believe good mentorship starts with clear communication. These general terms explain how to approach the information and resources shared through Monarch Codex. More specific terms may apply to individual services.

Education, mentorship and guidance

Monarch Codex shares educational content, mentorship and trading-related guidance intended to help members build knowledge and make informed decisions. Information may be general and may not reflect your personal financial circumstances. Where you need advice tailored to your situation, consider consulting an appropriately qualified professional.

Financial-market risk

Trading and investing involve risk, including the possibility of losing some or all of the money committed. Market conditions can change quickly. Signals, examples, strategies and educational materials are not guarantees of results, and past performance does not guarantee future outcomes. Make decisions carefully and only take risks you understand.

The Sovereign Desk

The Sovereign Desk is the signals and trading-guidance section associated with Monarch Codex. Access may depend on eligibility, payment or administrator approval. Contact official support to confirm access, fees and applicable terms. Do not rely on unofficial payment instructions or share passwords and private login codes.

The Online Cooperative

The Online Cooperative is a separate cooperative opportunity. Monarch Codex membership or participation in Monarch Codex services does not automatically enrol you in the cooperative. Review the cooperative’s official terms and contact its support for verified details about requirements, contributions, withdrawals or benefits.

Accounts and support

Use only official account and support links shared by Monarch Codex. Never share your password, one-time code or other private login credentials with anyone. If an account, payment or access detail is unclear, contact contactmonarchcodex@gmail.com before taking action.

Public profiles and member roles

Members may choose to publish a profile containing the display name, Monarch UID, profile image, location and roles they have elected to show. Do not publish another person’s image or personal information without permission. Public profiles should not expose private contact details, identity documents, account credentials or other sensitive information. A role badge reflects platform access or a role assigned by an authorised administrator; it is not a guarantee of expertise, professional qualification or financial performance. Members may hold more than one role, subject to the platform’s role and access rules.

Wallet transfers between members

If member-to-member wallet transfers are enabled for your account, use the recipient’s Monarch UID carefully and confirm the recipient details before approving a transfer. Transfers are subject to available balance, currency compatibility, account restrictions, security checks and any limits shown in the product. Never share passwords or one-time codes to arrange a transfer. Do not use wallet features for fraud, money laundering, coercion or any unlawful activity. Report an incorrect or suspicious transfer promptly through official support. Transfers may be subject to review and may not be reversible once completed.

Administrator roles and conduct

Administrators are appointed by authorised platform administrators and must use their permissions only for legitimate platform operations. Administrative access may be limited, reviewed, suspended or revoked. No administrator should request passwords, private login codes or off-platform payments in exchange for account access. Members must not impersonate Monarch Codex staff, misrepresent their assigned roles or attempt to bypass access controls.

Community conduct and uploaded content

Members are responsible for content they upload, including posts, comments and images. Do not upload unlawful, abusive, deceptive, infringing or privacy-violating material. Monarch Codex may remove content or restrict accounts where necessary to protect members, comply with law or enforce these rules. Only upload images you own or have permission to share.

Updates and availability

Services, learning materials, events and access arrangements may change. We will aim to communicate confirmed details clearly and will not publish unconfirmed events, prices or service terms as facts.
