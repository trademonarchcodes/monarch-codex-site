How can I join?

Visit the member registration and use the registration option available there.

What is the Academy?

The Academy is the learning section for trading and educational content. For class availability, dates, times and official class links, contact support.

What is The Sovereign Desk?

It is the full trading-support service associated with Monarch Codex, including signals, mentorship, announcements, private Desk chat and Telegram access for eligible active members. Full access costs $10 for 14 days. The weekly trade watchlist is available to signed-in members from their dashboard. Contact official support for payment instructions.

Are profits guaranteed?

No. Trading and investing involve risk, and profits are never guaranteed. A signal is not a promise or certainty of a market outcome. Only risk money you can afford to lose, and consider getting independent financial advice.

It is a separate network-based opportunity with package options and member milestones.

Follow the current instructions in your account or contact official support. We don’t publish unverified payment or withdrawal instructions here. Never send your password or private login codes to anyone.

Email support@monarchcodex.online. For account access, payments or approval, contact official support directly.

Can I join the trading class?

Yes, you can ask about it, but the confirmed date, time and official class link have not been provided on this page. Contact support to confirm details.
